Image sources for the introduction to control and machine learning
===================================================================

All pictures come from the PowerPoint deck `slides-master-part1-ppt.pptx` (E. Zuazua,
October 2026) from which these slides were converted. They were renamed, and the large
screenshots were converted to JPEG or capped at 2000 px wide, with no other change.
Formulas and text that the deck showed as screenshots of older beamer slides are
typeset natively in the `.tex` instead.

Pictures
--------

- `ai_letters.png`, `ai_chip.jpg`, `ai_circuit_icon.png`, `ai_brain_gears.jpg`,
  `ai_brain_dark.jpg`: stock illustrations of "AI" used on the opening slides.
- `nobel_chemistry_2024.png`, `hinton_nobel_physics_2024.png`: screenshots of
  [nobelprize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/) (Chemistry 2024 popular summary;
  Geoffrey Hinton, Physics 2024). `deepseek_chatgpt.png`: press photo of the two logos.
- `its_a_bubble_cartoon.jpg`: cartoon "It's a bubble" (cavemen around a fire), of
  unknown authorship, as circulated on social media.
- `this_person_does_not_exist.png`: a generated face from
  [this-person-does-not-exist.com](https://this-person-does-not-exist.com).
- `wikipedia_computer_animated_hand.png`: screenshot of the Wikipedia article
  [A Computer Animated Hand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Computer_Animated_Hand).
- `face_wireframe.jpg`: a polygonal face mesh; `face_mask_mba.jpg`: the "RF mask"
  proportion diagram (© MBA).
- `peyre_tutte_embedding.png`: screenshot of three vignettes of Gabriel Peyré's
  [Mathematical Nexus](https://www.gpeyre.com/mathematical-nexus/) on Tutte embeddings.
- `keenan_crane_webpage.png`: screenshot of [Keenan Crane's homepage](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/).
- `hallucinating_robot.jpg`: illustration of an "AI hallucinating" (stock image).
- `dali_temptation_st_anthony.jpg`: Salvador Dalí, *The Temptation of Saint Anthony*
  (1946), Royal Museums of Fine Arts of Belgium; reproduction as circulated online.
- `escher_relativity.jpg`: M. C. Escher, *Relativity* (1953).
  `escher_moebius_strip_ii.jpg`: M. C. Escher, *Möbius Strip II* (1963).
- `klein_bottle.jpg`: rendering of a Klein bottle.
- `aristotle_politics_cover.jpg`: cover of an edition of Aristotle's *Politics*.
- `murray_2003_control_collage.png`: collage from *Control in an Information Rich
  World*, SIAM, R. Murray (ed.), 2003.
- `maxwell_on_governors_text.png`, `maxwell_on_governors_header.png`: excerpts of
  J. C. Maxwell, *On Governors*, Proc. Royal Society of London 16 (1868), 270–283
  ([scan](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/maxwell1.pdf)); public domain.
  `centrifugal_governor_diagram.jpg`: labelled diagram of a centrifugal governor.
- `steam_engine.jpg`, `steam_locomotive.png`, `pressure_cooker.png`: stock photos.
- `wiener.jpg`: portrait of Norbert Wiener. `kalman.jpg`: portrait of Rudolf E. Kálmán.
- `pagerank_page_brin.png`: photo of Sergey Brin and Larry Page next to the PageRank
  example graph of the Wikipedia article [PageRank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PageRank).
- `markov_wikipedia_infobox.png`: infobox of the Wikipedia article
  [Andrey Markov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Markov).
- `hairer_wanner_cover.jpg`: cover of E. Hairer, G. Wanner, *Analysis by Its History*,
  Springer, 1996.
- `nelson_car_model_sontag.png`: Figure 4.1 of E. Sontag, *Mathematical Control
  Theory*, 2nd ed., Springer, 1998.
- `supervised_learning_network.png`: diagram of a residual network used for
  supervised learning (from the older beamer slides of the course).
- `classification_blobs.png`: crop of the same slides, two point clouds to classify.
- `legendre.jpg`, `gauss.png`, `least_squares_fits.png`: portraits and figures of the
  Wikipedia article [Least squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Least_squares).
- `wikipedia_sampling.png`: screenshot of the Wikipedia article
  [Sampling (statistics)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampling_(statistics)).
- `sutton_bitter_lesson_tweet.png`: screenshot of a post by Richard Sutton (May 2026)
  summarizing [The Bitter Lesson](http://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html).
